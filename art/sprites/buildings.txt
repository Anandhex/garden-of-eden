# Buildings (G6): the tiles the templates (art/tiled/templates) dress a building with, 16×16 each, plus the town
# walls, doors and gates the map itself sends. A roof is three rows of tiles: `top` (the ridge, standing over the
# street behind), `mid` (repeated for deep buildings) and `near` (down to the eave); each in _l, _m and _r. The
# front wall is the building's last row. `base=` draws over an earlier tile (`.` keeps it, `,` clears it, the last
# row repeats). `window` marks a wall tile with a window (it is lit after dark when someone is home); `glow=` names
# the lit-window frame drawn over it.
