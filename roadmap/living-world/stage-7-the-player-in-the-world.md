# Stage 7: The player in the world

What the player sees, the life they lead, the world reaching out to them, and what the world remembers.

## N65: The player's view

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

**Goal:** the player can see the living world they know about, without the admin view.

**The rules:** everything shown is what the player knows (N24's rule): heard, seen or been told, with how sure they are.

- [ ] A journal: news heard (with who told them and how reliable), stories (N20) they know, quests, their own deeds
- [ ] People they know: who they are, where they were last seen, how they feel about the player as far as the player can tell, their family and allegiances as known
- [ ] A world map that fills in: places visited or heard of, roads (N63), borders and wars as last heard
- [ ] Places: what the player knows of each (head, mood, prices, dangers)
- [ ] Tests, README, this roadmap

- **Done when:** after a week of play the journal lists the rumours the player heard with their sources, the map shows a far city only once someone has told them of it, and a person's page shows a stale location until the player learns where they went.

## N66: The player's own life

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

**Goal:** the player lives in the world like its people: a home, property, work, a family, age and heirs.

**The rules:** the same rules as NPCs (N25, N40, N45) apply to the player where they fit.

- [ ] Rent or buy a house; own land, a shop or a workshop; hire people to run it (wages, theft, loyalty)
- [ ] Run a business: buy, make and sell through N10's economy; taxes to the place's head (N13)
- [ ] Court, marry and have children (N11's rules); a spouse and children with their own lives
- [ ] Age; play on as an heir after death, inheriting by the kingdom's law (N45), with the world's memory of the parent
- [ ] Tests, README, this roadmap

- **Done when:** the player buys a workshop in Millbrook, hires an apprentice who runs it while they travel, marries and has a child, and after the player's death plays on as that child with the workshop and the family's reputation.

## N67: NPCs come to you, and reputation by place

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

**Goal:** the world reaches out to the player, and what they've done follows them, place by place.

**The rules:** N38's menu gains "seek out the player": an NPC who needs something the player can give, wants to warn or threaten them, or has an offer, travels to find them. Reputation is kept per place and per faction, and spreads by N33's carriers.

- [ ] NPCs seek out the player with requests, offers, warnings, threats or debts to collect; quests come from people turning up
- [ ] Reputation per place and faction: a hero in one town, wanted in the next; bounties that travel
- [ ] People react to the player's renown, gear and deeds when they meet them
- [ ] Tests, README, this roadmap

- **Done when:** an NPC whose son the player saved travels to find them with a reward, a gang whose leader the player killed sends someone after them, and the player is welcome in Millbrook but turned away at Highdale's gate after a crime there.

## N68: Legends, songs and the realm's history

**Status:** ⬜ Planned

**Goal:** the world remembers its past.

**The rules:** big events and N20's stories become legends: code picks them and keeps the facts; the model writes the song or tale from those facts (invented-name checks apply). Each retelling drifts, like N7's rumours.

- [ ] Bards who make songs of great deeds and disasters and sing them in inns; NPCs refer to them years later
- [ ] Named eras ("the Ashford Revolt", "the Long Winter") from the biggest stories
- [ ] A chronicle of the realm the player can read (in the game, what's known; in the admin view, the truth)
- [ ] Monuments and place names from events (the Weeping Bridge, Greyfang's Hollow)
- [ ] Tests, README, this roadmap

- **Done when:** a revolt becomes a song sung in a Highdale inn within a season, its details drift with retelling, the era is named in the chronicle, and an NPC years later refers to it.
