# N32 plan: Interiors and homes

> **N26H (planned 2026-10-24) builds steps 1 and 2 of this plan as its step 0**, with real building footprints (a cottage 4×3, a castle keep in a 30×24 bailey, a palace a 40×30 compound) and an access rule on every door (a home is private: only the household and people they let in). If N26H is built first, start this plan at step 3 and build trespass on N26H's `canEnter`.

**In one line:** every building's door leads to an interior map generated from (place seed, building index, kind, the household's class, wealth and trade); a household's belongings are a few compact rows that become real items only when touched; the crowd already knows who is home, so the interior shows them eating, sleeping or working; trespass is noticed by whoever is inside.

**Read first:** [plans README](README.md) sections 2, 9, 15, 18. Needs N16 (the roster: homes, workplaces, classes, jobs; the crowd's positions), N18 (people rows), N31 (fixed layouts, so building index *i* is always the same building; marks show damage inside). Hooks for N46 (secrets in a drawer), N48 (prisons), N66 (the player's home).

## What exists

- Buildings on site maps have `kind` (house, smithy, inn, temple, market, hall, guildhall, barracks, mill…), a lot, a door tile (`door` tile type in [`web/map.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/web/map.ts)), and townsfolk have `home` (a house index) and `work` ([`realm/townsfolk.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/realm/townsfolk.ts), the shared `Townsperson`).
- The crowd ([`shared/crowd.ts`](../../../packages/shared/src/crowd.ts)) puts a person "inside" a building as a position at its door or hidden; activities ("asleep", "eating", "at work") are known per person per minute.
- Theft and stealth ([`stealth.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/stealth.ts): `steal`, `sneak`, `caughtStealing`, `recognisesStolen`), crime and guards ([`crime.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/crime.ts): witnesses, `reportCrimes`, bounties).
- Talkers have secrets ([`npc/secrets.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/npc/secrets.ts): `reachableSecrets`, `learnSecret`) and depth from N25a (`npc/depth/`), but the secrets are words, not things in a house.
- Areas: the player moves between `area`s of a site (`village`, `woods`, `camp`, `mine`, `road`) with `siteMap(s, area, realm)`.

## Decisions made in this plan

1. **An interior is an area.** Entering a door switches the player to area `in:<buildingIndex>` of the same site, so movement, the clock, saving, the crowd and talking all work as for other areas. `siteMap(s, "in:12", realm)` returns the interior; the exit is the door back to the village at the building's front.
2. **Generated by a pure function in shared code.** `interiorOf(seed, site, buildingIndex, kind, household)` in `packages/shared/src/interior/` → tiles (floor, wall, door, window), rooms, furniture (bed, table, hearth, chest, shelves, loom, anvil, altar, bar, cell…), with the household's class and wealth choosing size, number of rooms and furniture quality. A slave's or serf's hovel: one room, a hearth, straw beds. A merchant's house: three rooms, a strongbox, a good table. A noble's hall: a hall, a solar, servants' quarters. Workplaces by trade (a smithy's forge and anvil, a weaver's loom). The browser makes the same map (no tiles sent).
3. **Wealth changes furniture, not layout.** The layout (walls, rooms) is fixed by the seed and the building's size; the **furnishing** reads the household's wealth band at the time (a new table after a good year, bare rooms in famine, burned corners from N31 marks). Bands change rarely (computed daily, hysteresis), so the house doesn't flicker.
4. **Belongings are compact.** A household has a **stash list**: `household_goods(site, building, slot, item, qty, hidden, owner, ref)`, made lazily the first time anyone (the player, a thief NPC, a fire) touches the building, rolled from the household's class, trade and wealth with `streamFor(seed, "goods", site, building)`. Until then nothing is stored. A slot is a piece of furniture (the chest, the desk, under a floorboard). `hidden` slots need a search check (perception) to find. Taking an item makes it a real inventory item and records a theft event if it wasn't yours.
5. **Secrets as things.** A talker with a secret of a kind that leaves a trace (a letter, a ledger, a stolen item, a runaway's papers: N46 makes these typed) gets a **hidden slot** in their home or workplace holding a document item that carries the secret's ref. Reading it is `learnSecret` for the player (and a "discovered" event for N46). Until N46, use the depth facts' secrets and one document per talker with a secret.
6. **People indoors are the crowd.** The interior draws the household members whose crowd position is "inside this building" now, at a furniture spot by activity (asleep → bed, eating → table, working → workplace furniture, quarrelling → two at the table with a bubble from N37 if built). Talkers inside are the same talkers.
7. **Trespass is a crime with witnesses.** Entering a home that isn't yours, a shop after hours or a locked door is **trespass** (new crime kind). Doors are locked at night and when the household is out (by class: hovels are never locked; merchants and nobles always lock); picking a lock is a check with the existing `lockpicking` skill ([`npc/skills.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/npc/skills.ts)). Anyone inside or passing the door who sees the player rolls to notice (sneak vs perception); a servant or a resident who notices shouts, and the crime system calls the guard.
8. **Prisons and guild halls** get their interiors with cells and offices; N48 puts prisoners in cells. The player's home (N66) is a building the player owns, with furnishings the player places (a later phase; the slot model already allows it).

## Steps

### Step 1: interiors as areas
- Area ids `in:<i>`; `siteMap` returns interiors; door tiles in the village are walkable into them; the exit returns to the door. Server move validation for interiors.
- Test: entering and leaving a house puts the player inside then back at the door; the interior is the same on two visits.

### Step 2: the generator
- `shared/src/interior/generate.ts` with layouts by building kind and class; furniture tables; quality by wealth band.
- Test: a serf's house and a merchant's house in the start town differ in size, rooms and furniture quality; the same house generated twice is byte-equal (shared test, and a server/client equality test as N26's chunks have).

### Step 3: the browser
- `client/src/world/InteriorLayer.ts` (or the site tilemap in interior mode): walls, floor, furniture sprites, a dimmer view; textures.
- Play: walk into a hovel, a merchant's house, the inn, the temple, the smithy.

### Step 4: people inside
- From the crowd: residents at furniture spots by activity; talkers inside are clickable as usual.
- Test: at 02:00 the household of a house is in its beds; at noon a worker isn't home.

### Step 5: belongings and hidden things
- `household_goods` (schema migration), lazy roll, search action, take action (theft event), quality and items by class and trade.
- Documents with secret refs in talkers' homes or workplaces (decision 5).
- Test: searching the start town's head's desk (the "mayor"'s home: the head's house) finds a hidden letter whose ref is one of their real secrets; reading it makes the player know the secret.

### Step 6: trespass and locks
- Crime kind `trespass`; locked doors by class, time and presence; `lockpicking` checks; noticing; the alarm and the guard.
- Test: entering the head's house at night, a servant (a household member of class servant/slave, or any resident awake) notices on a failed sneak and the guard is called (a crime opened with the servant as witness).

### Step 7: change
- Wealth band per household (daily, with hysteresis) → furniture quality; N31 marks inside (burned corners); famine → bare shelves.
- Test: the same house's furniture after a good year and after a famine differs.

### Step 8: docs
- README: entering buildings, trespass, searching.

## Balance bounds

- `goods-bounded`: `household_goods` rows only for touched buildings; stays small over a long run.

## Edge cases and traps

- **Talkers' "places"** ([`schedule.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/schedule.ts) `PlaceId`: the forge, the inn) predate building interiors. Map each talker place to a building index (their workplace or home) so that "Brena is at the forge" and "Brena is inside the smithy" agree. Don't keep two ideas of where someone is.
- **The crowd's "inside" is a door position today.** Inside an interior, the crowd sim must give positions *in the interior*; outside, residents inside aren't drawn. Keep this in shared code so the browser agrees.
- **Interiors and time:** standing inside passes time as anywhere; sleeping in your own bed (N66) or the inn's (N26) restores needs.
- **Never send the true contents of hidden slots** to the client before they're found.
- **Prisons** must not be enterable freely; the door is a guarded, locked door.
