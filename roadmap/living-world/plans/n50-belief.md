# N50 plan: Belief

**In one line:** faiths are realm factions (temples, orders, cults) with **tenets** that change how followers score actions; every person has a faith and a devotion; faiths spread by preaching and gossip and convert people; a kingdom's **state faith** and **tolerance** policies decide heresy and persecution; **omens** are events rolled in code from what's happening, read by seers and priests in their faction's interest, and a court seer's reading can tip a ruler's decision; holy sites draw pilgrims; in a crisis people look for someone to blame.

**Read first:** [plans README](README.md) sections 2, 13, 14 (`state_faith`, `tolerance`), 15, 19 (`creed`, `sermon`). Needs N33 (gossip carries faith), N38 (the menu: pray, preach, convert, persecute), N48 (heresy trials). Uses N26 (pilgrims are already journeys), N28 (sacred species and omens from birds), N30 (festivals), N36 (fear makes people devout), N58 (rulers' choices that omens tip), N61 (blame in a plague).

## What exists

- **Faction kinds** include `cult` and `order` ([`realm/types.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/realm/types.ts) `FACTION_KINDS`); factions have `creed` (a few words, model-named by the director through `nameFaction`), members, seats, branches, stances.
- **Temples** are a building (`temple`) with priests (role `priest`, class `clergy`, job `priest`/`acolyte`).
- **Pilgrims** are a journey source in N26 ([`road/sources.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/road/sources.ts)) going to temples.
- **The realm director** ([`realm/director.ts`](../../../packages/server/src/game/realm/director.ts)) founds factions for needs.
- **Nothing** gives a person a faith or a devotion; nothing reads a creed.

## Decisions made in this plan

1. **Faiths are factions with tenets.** A faith = a realm faction of kind `order` (an established church), `cult` (a small or secret faith) or a new kind `temple` if needed, plus `faith: { tenets: Tenet[], deity: string, sacred: { species?, places?, days? }, rites: string[], orthodox?: factionId }`. Tenets are data (`data/tenets/*.json`): each modifies N38 score parts for followers (`charity`: +help, −steal; `martial`: +enlist, +confront; `ascetic`: −leisure, −drink; `obedience`: +obey, −refuse_order, −revolt; `liberation`: +help runaways, +revolt against masters; `purity`: +persecute; `mercy`: −execute, +pardon), and some forbid acts (sorcery, eating a sacred animal). A realm starts with one or two established faiths and a few cults, generated by culture (N53 later) and region.
2. **Each person has a faith and a devotion.** Rows: `faith` (faction id) and `devotion` 0..1 (two columns); talkers the same on the state. Assigned at generation by place (the local temple's faith, with a share of cultists), inherited from parents at birth, changed by conversion. Devotion rises with fear, grief (N36) and the priest's influence, falls with prosperity and scandal.
3. **Spreading:** preaching is an N38 action (priests, cultists, zealous followers) in venues (N33): a conversion chance by the preacher's speechcraft and renown, the listener's devotion to their current faith (low = open), grievances that match the tenets (the poor listen to `charity` preachers), the place's mood, and the state's tolerance. Gossip carries faith news (a miracle, a scandal: knowledge refs that move devotion). Cults spread along roads with travellers (missionaries as journeys; N26).
4. **Heresy and persecution** are policies: `state_faith` (a faith id or none) and `tolerance` (`free` | `tolerated` | `taxed` | `banned` | `persecuted`) per faith. Banned faiths' members hide (their faith becomes a `cult` secret: N46); persecution is an N38 action for zealous followers of the state faith and for guards under a crackdown: denounce (a case: N48 `heresy`), burn a shrine (marks: N31), attack (violence on). Persecuted cults may become martyrs (devotion up among the rest) or flee (migration) or rise (N56's bands with a faith grievance).
5. **Omens are events.** `omensDay(realm, day)` rolls rare signs from the world's state: an eclipse or comet (pure hash of the day, realm-wide), a two-headed calf (in a farming place), birds flying the wrong way (N28), a bleeding statue (where devotion is high and mood is fearful), a plague's start, a storm on a festival. Each is a realm news item. **Readings:** seers and priests interpret an omen in their faction's interest (a table: the same comet is a sign of war to a martial order, of judgment to a purity cult, of a new ruler to a plotter); the reading is a short structured claim `{ omen, means: "war" | "peace" | "repent" | "purge" | "new_ruler" | "bad_harvest" …, target? }`.
6. **Seers tip rulers.** Each court has a seer (a talker or figure of a faith; a court wizard later: N51). When a ruler faces a decision with a policy choice (N45's `decidePolicy`: war or peace, repression or concession, persecution or tolerance), the latest relevant reading adds a pressure term weighted by the ruler's devotion and the seer's standing. The decision's `why` names it ("…after the court seer read the comet as a call to war").
7. **Pilgrimages:** holy sites (a temple with a relic, a grove, standing stones: N27's POIs, sacred by a faith) hold festivals (N30's calendar, per faith); devout followers within reach go as pilgrims (N26's source, now by faith and devotion), with trade and inns busier along the way.
8. **Blame in a crisis:** in a famine, plague (N61) or disaster (N62), a scapegoat is chosen by rule: a minority faith with low tolerance, or a cult recently grown; the blame is gossip, raises persecution scores, and may make a ruler change tolerance (with a why). Logged as cause.
9. **Words:** creeds and sermons from templates by tenet; the model may write a creed (`creed`) once per new faith, and sermon lines for the player to hear (`sermon`, low priority).

## Steps

### Step 1: faiths, tenets, people's faith
- Faith data on factions; tenet files; generation; rows' `faith`/`devotion` (migration); talkers' faith; inheritance at birth.
- Test: every person has a faith; the shares follow the place's temple.

### Step 2: tenets in scoring
- N38 score parts from tenets; forbidden acts.
- Test: a devout follower of a charity faith gives more and steals less than a follower of a martial faith in the same spot.

### Step 3: spreading and conversion
- `preach` action, conversion rules, missionaries' journeys, faith news in gossip.
- Test (done-when part): a cult spreads from one town to the next (members in the second town after a missionary journey and some weeks).

### Step 4: policies, heresy, persecution
- `state_faith`, `tolerance` per faith; denounce/burn/attack actions; heresy cases; hiding as secrets; martyrdom, flight.
- Test (done-when part): the cult is persecuted as heresy in a harsh kingdom (cases opened, a shrine burned).

### Step 5: omens and readings
- `omensDay`; readings by faction; seers at courts; the pressure term in `decidePolicy`.
- Test (done-when part): an omen read by a court seer changes a ruler's decision (same seed with and without the omen: different policy; the `why` names the reading).

### Step 6: pilgrimages
- Holy sites; faith festivals; pilgrims by faith and devotion.
- Test (done-when part): pilgrims travel to a shrine at its festival.

### Step 7: blame; words; admin; docs
- Scapegoating rule; creeds and sermons; admin: faiths by place and kingdom, policies, omens and readings. README.

## Balance bounds

- `faiths-persist`: no faith wiped out in a normal run unless persecuted hard (then rare); the established faith keeps a majority in most places.
- `omens-rare`: a few per year in the realm.
- `persecution-bounded`.

## Edge cases and traps

- **Tenets must stay small modifiers**; a faith shouldn't turn a town into saints or killers. Bound the parts (|tenet part| ≤ 0.3).
- **Seers and determinism:** the reading is a table lookup by faction and omen, not a model call; the model only writes the words.
- **Secret faith (banned)** is an N46 secret: discovery rules apply.
