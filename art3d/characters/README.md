# Garden of Eden base character (Blender 4.3)

One parametric body for every person: a single shared topology (masculine at `Sex` 0, feminine at `Sex` 1), 63 blend
shapes (incl. four species keys), a Unity-Humanoid rig, split at hidden seams into swappable parts. Built on the CC0
**Blender Studio Human Base Meshes** (stylised male and female) with scripts; see `CREDITS.md`.
Look: `roadmap/game/plans/g8-art-direction.md` (soft stylised 3D, about 7 heads, long legs, slender neck, slightly large head,
room for large almond eyes and full lips). Reference qualities: `images/2.webp` (stylised 3D mage), not copied.

## Rebuild

```
B=/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender
$B -b -P art3d/characters/build_base.py                  # base.blend + export/*   (about 10 s, deterministic)
$B -b art3d/characters/base.blend -P art3d/characters/render_preview.py          # all previews (about 70 s)
$B -b art3d/characters/base.blend -P art3d/characters/render_preview.py -- only=species,faces,seams,grid,poses,neutral
$B -b -P art3d/characters/verify_export.py               # re-imports the FBX; bones, blend shapes, seam checks
```

Files: `source/human_base_stylized.blend` (+ the bundle's README/licence text), `extract_source.py` (how it was cut from the
bundle), `source_mesh.py` (read, proportion, decimate, share topology), `fieldmap.py` (virtual space for the shape fields),
`shapes.py` (59 shapes as displacement fields), `species.py` (4 species keys, presets), `build_base.py` (pipeline),
`render_preview.py`, `verify_export.py`, `common.py`. Outputs: `base.blend`, `export/base_body.fbx`,
`export/base_body_meta.json`, `export/joint_offsets.json`, `export/species_presets.json`, `previews/*.png`.

## What came from the bundle, what is generated

| From Blender Studio Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 (CC0) | Generated by the scripts here |
|---|---|
| the stylised male and female full bodies (head, face with eyelids/lips/ears/nose, hands with fingers, feet with toes; 12,500 quads each, identical topology) | proportion warp (head 25 % smaller, longer legs/neck, 1.75 m / 1.65 m, 7 heads), per-part decimation to 13.3k tris, one shared topology for both sexes |
| the artist's face sets (regions with clean boundary loops) = the seam loops and the finger/palm/toe/ear/nose regions | rig (joints taken from the mesh's own region boundaries), weights, UVs, sockets |
| the stylised eyeball meshes | 58 blend shapes (fields), 4 species keys; `Sex` is the exact male-to-female difference of the bundle's two meshes |
| the male/female shapes themselves | joint offsets, species presets, elf ear / nose variants, orc tusks, all preview shaders and the face/eye decal texture |

## Parts (separate mesh objects on one armature; seams are the artist's face-set loops)

| Object | Region and seam | Tris | Keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Mesh_Head` | skull, face, eyelids, lips and neck; seam at the base of the neck | 4106 | 57 |
| `Mesh_Torso` | chest, abdomen, pelvis and the shoulder caps; seams: neck, shoulder caps, thighs | 1334 | 23 |
| `Mesh_Arms` | upper arm below the shoulder cap to the wrist | 1276 | 17 |
| `Mesh_Hands` | palm and 15 phalanges, cut at the wrist | 1848 | 14 |
| `Mesh_Legs` | thighs to just above the ankle (pelvis/briefs-line seam) | 2256 | 15 |
| `Mesh_Feet` | ankle, foot, toes | 1542 | 10 |
| `Part_Nose` | nose, a hole in the head filled by the part (seam loop of 30 shared verts) | 314 | 41 |
| `Part_Ear_L`, `Part_Ear_R` | ears (22 shared verts each) | 292 each | 15 |
| `Part_Eye_L`, `Part_Eye_R` | stylised eyeballs (not welded; they sit in the eyelid sockets, skinned to the eye bones) | 352 each | 16 |

Body parts total **13,260 tris / 6,942 verts**, plus 704 for the eyes (14.0k). Mixed quads and triangles. The FBX also
contains the species variants (about 2.8k more tris) which are never shown together with the defaults.

Seams (checked by `verify_export.py` on the re-imported FBX): every seam vertex is a bit-identical copy: same position, same
weights (max diff 0), same delta in every shape key (max diff 0), same custom normal (diff about 2e-4 = float rounding).
Part boundaries are locked during decimation, so both sides keep exactly the same vertices. See `previews/seams_closeups.png`
(neck, wrist, hip, ankle; neutral, mixed, heavy+old) and `previews/parts_colour_coded.png`.
- Normals are custom split normals frozen from the welded mesh: import with **Normals = Import**, **Blend Shape Normals = None**.
  Under extreme shape stacks the frozen normals can show a faint line along the hip/neck seam (see weak spots).
- Swappable nose/ears: a replacement nose or ear must keep the boundary loop of the default part (same vertex count and
  positions on the loop); everything inside is free. Variants here are made exactly like that (displaced interior, locked
  boundary, normals blended back to the head at the rim) and carry the head's shape keys, so they follow every face shape.
- Clothes follow the same rule: replace a whole part and keep its boundary loops, or build the garment on the neutral body
  and copy weights and shape keys from the nearest body vertex (the transfer used for ears, noses and eyes).

Rest pose is the bundle's **A-pose** (arms about 35 degrees from vertical). Unity Humanoid accepts it (set the avatar T-pose
once if the muscle ranges look off); an A-pose keeps shoulders natural in every arms-down pose and suits armour fitting.

## Blend shapes (all 0..1, additive, symmetric)

Body (18): `Sex` (0 masculine, 1 feminine, exact), `Height_Legs`, `Shoulders`, `Chest_Muscle`, `Breast`, `Waist_Slim`,
`Waist_Wide`, `Belly`, `Hips`, `Butt`, `Arms_Thick`, `Legs_Thick`, `Muscle`, `Thin`, `Heavy`, `Age_Old`, `Child`, `Neck_Long`.
Face shape (10): `Face_Long`, `Face_Short`, `Jaw_Wide`, `Jaw_Square`, `Cheeks_Full`, `Cheekbones`, `Forehead_High`, `Brow_Ridge`,
`Nose_Bridge`, `Ears_Back`. Eyes (12): `Eye_Big`, `Eye_Spacing`, `Eye_Round`, `Eye_Almond`, `Eye_Narrow`, `Eye_Hooded`,
`Eye_Monolid`, `Eye_TiltUp`, `Eye_TiltDown`, `Eye_Deep`, `Eye_Protrude`, `Eye_Height` (they act on the eyelid/socket geometry of
`Mesh_Head`; the eyeballs follow the socket centre). Chin (9): `Chin_Pointed`, `Chin_Long`, `Chin_Small`, `Chin_Big`, `Chin_Wide`,
`Chin_Narrow`, `Chin_Forward`, `Chin_Back`, `Chin_Cleft`. Lips/mouth (10): `Lips_Full`, `Lips_UpperFull`, `Lips_LowerFull`,
`Lips_Thin`, `Lips_CupidBow`, `Lips_Pout`, `Mouth_Wide`, `Mouth_Narrow`, `Mouth_CornersUp`, `Mouth_CornersDown`.
Species (4): `Species_Elf`, `Species_Dwarf`, `Species_Halfling`, `Species_Orc` (human = all species keys 0).

How they are made: `shapes.py` describes each as a displacement field over a virtual upright 1.75 m T-pose adult; `fieldmap.py`
maps every vertex of the real A-pose body into that space (arms rotated up and rescaled, landmark z-map, x/y scale), evaluates
the field there and maps the delta back (inverse Jacobian; the shoulder pivot's own movement is not rotated). Deltas are
Laplacian-smoothed around the shoulder join so shoulders stay clean. Only keys that move a part are exported on it.
Order of magnitude to stay clean: sum of weights touching one region <= about 2.

### Joint offsets
Blend shapes move skin, not bones. `export/joint_offsets.json` lists, per shape, how far each bone head moves at weight 1
(nearest-vertex delta; Blender axes, Unity = (-x, z, -y)). Runtime: apply blend shapes, set each bone's world position to
`rest + sum(weight * offset)`, then recompute the renderers' `bindposes`
(`bindpose[i] = bone[i].worldToLocalMatrix * renderer.localToWorld`). The preview poses do the equivalent (rotation pivots
move by the offsets).

## Species (talking peoples of `packages/server/src/world/species.ts`)

Same rig, topology, seams, blend-shape names and sockets as the human; a species is a preset in `export/species_presets.json`:
shape weights (`Species_<id>` = a fixed mix of the existing body/face keys plus a few species-only fields), a uniform root
scale to reach the target height, skin colour ranges and which swappable parts it uses.

| id | height M / F | root scale M | what the key does | swappable parts | skin (display colours) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| human | 1.75 / 1.65 | 1.00 | base | `Part_Ear_L/R`, `Part_Nose` | warm to deep brown |
| elf | 1.95 / 1.84 | 1.07 | slender, longer neck and limbs, finer face, cheekbones, upturned almond eyes, longer hands | `Part_Ear_Elf_{A,B,C}_{L,R}` (short, long swept, leaf), `Part_Nose_Elf` | pale to warm sand |
| dwarf | 1.30 / 1.23 | 0.78 | broad shoulders and chest, thick limbs, short legs, heavy brow and jaw, bigger hands | `Part_Nose_Dwarf` (big nose); beards later | ruddy |
| halfling | 1.05 / 0.99 | 0.66 | child-like proportions with an adult face, round cheeks, larger feet | default ears and nose | warm |
| orc | 2.05 / 1.93 | 1.20 | bigger and broader, heavy brow, square jaw, underbite (chin forward), flat nose, big hands | `Part_Nose_Orc`, `Part_Tusk_{A,B}_{L,R}` | green to grey-green |

`rootScaleF` is also in the JSON. Previews: `species_neutral_front.png`, `species_neutral_q34.png` (masculine row over feminine
row, true relative heights), `species_faces.png`, `species_variety_<id>.png` (8 people each: body types, age, face mixes,
alternating masculine/feminine).
Free resources searched first (see `CREDITS.md`): nothing CC0 with the same rig/topology exists for elf ears, orc tusks or
halfling/dwarf bodies (free Gumroad elf ears have unclear licences and need checkout; Sloyd CC-BY characters have their own rigs),
so the ear/nose variants and tusks are generated from our own parts. Strong beards, hair and clothes: MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 packs are
the next place to look.

## UVs and materials
One shared 0..1 atlas (v up). **Face decal rect: u 0.0..0.5, v 0.5..1.0**, planar front projection of the head and nose front
(1,811 faces): `u = (x + 0.11) / 0.22 * 0.5`, `v = 0.5 + (z - z0) / 0.22 * 0.5`, z0 = chin - 0.005 (1.50 m for the neutral
masculine; the same texture rect for every shape, the decals deform with the face). Brows, lash lines, blush and lip tint are
decals (`render_preview.py: face_texture()` paints an example from the stored landmarks, base colour white so the skin tint
multiplies). Eyeballs: planar front projection into the top-right quadrant (left eye u 0.5..0.75, right 0.75..1, v 0.75..1,
iris centred). All other skin: smart-projected islands packed in the lower-right quadrant (u 0.5..1, v 0..0.5), no overlaps.
Materials: `Skin` on every part, `Face` on the decal region of head and nose, `Eye` on the eyeballs. Vertex group `Face_Decal`.

**Face decal fix (2026-11):** the example lash line used to run from the inner corner across the nose bridge and joined both eyes (it
read as glasses). The lid arc is now computed from the measured lid aperture of the neutral head (ray test: centre x 0.036, half width
0.0165, half height 0.0105, z 1.6255), stops before the inner corner and is hard-masked to the eye area. The swappable decals
(eyes/lids, brows, lips, stubble) live in `parts/` (`parts/decals.py`) and follow the same rule; see `parts/README.md`.

## Rig
55 bones, Unity Humanoid names, no extra bones in the chain, Hips is the only root; Blender left is +X, front -Y. Joints come
from the mesh's own region boundaries (elbow = upper/lower arm boundary, wrist, knee, ankle, every finger joint from the
phalange face sets, ball of the foot from the toe sets). Weights: Blender automatic weights, 3 smoothing passes (not hands),
Jaw takes up to 90 % of Head weight on the lower face, max 4 influences; eyeballs 100 % on `LeftEye`/`RightEye`.

| Our bone | Nearest SOMA/SMPL-style joint |
|---|---|
| Hips | pelvis (SMPL 0) |
| Spine / Chest / UpperChest | spine1 / spine2 / spine3 (SMPL 3 / 6 / 9) |
| Neck, Head | neck (12), head (15) |
| Jaw, LeftEye, RightEye | jaw, left_eye, right_eye (SMPL-X) |
| LeftShoulder / RightShoulder | left_collar / right_collar (13 / 14) |
| LeftUpperArm / RightUpperArm | left_shoulder / right_shoulder (16 / 17) |
| LeftLowerArm / RightLowerArm | left_elbow / right_elbow (18 / 19) |
| LeftHand / RightHand | left_wrist / right_wrist (20 / 21) |
| Left/Right Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Little Proximal, Intermediate, Distal | finger joints 1-3 of each finger (SMPL-X hands) |
| LeftUpperLeg / RightUpperLeg | left_hip / right_hip (1 / 2) |
| LeftLowerLeg / RightLowerLeg | left_knee / right_knee (4 / 5) |
| LeftFoot / RightFoot | left_ankle / right_ankle (7 / 8) |
| LeftToes / RightToes | left_foot / right_foot (10 / 11) |

Retarget notes: SMPL's rest pose is an A-pose with arms about 45 degrees down, ours about 35; SOMA joint names are not in this repo,
map by the SMPL indices.

## Export
`export/base_body.fbx`: Armature, 9 body parts, 2 eyes, 13 species parts, 16 empties. Verified by re-import: 55 bones (all
Humanoid, none extra), 63 blend shapes, Armature modifier and custom normals on every mesh, seam diffs above. Settings: forward -Z,
up Y, `apply_unit_scale`, `FBX_SCALE_ALL`, global scale 1, no leaf bones, primary Y / secondary X, armature node Null, shape keys as
BlendShapes. Unity: Rig = Humanoid, Normals = Import, Blend Shape Normals = None, Read/Write on, Scale Factor 1 (1 unit = 1 m).
Blender front (-Y) becomes +Z, Blender left (+X) becomes -X. For a species: set its shape weights, then root scale (`rootScaleM/F`).

## Weak spots
- Normals are frozen at rest: extreme stacks (e.g. `Heavy` + `Age_Old`) show a faint straight line along the hip and neck seams and a
  small pinched triangle at the crotch (see the bottom row of `seams_closeups.png`). `Child` (and halfling) leave slightly
  flappy shoulder caps.
- Face resolution is moderate (head 4.1k tris): `Chin_Cleft`, `Lips_CupidBow` and the lid keys are subtle. No inner mouth, teeth
  or tongue; eyelids are sculpted, not rigged.
- Species keys are mixes plus a few fields, not sculpts: dwarf/halfling hands and feet are only scaled, orc skin and
  teeth are placeholders; `Species_*` stacked with strong body keys is untuned.
- A-pose rest pose (Humanoid avatar needs a quick check in Unity).
- Elf ear and nose variants are displaced copies of the human parts, not new sculpts.
- The preview shader is a Blender shader-to-RGB trick; Unity needs its own shader.
