Everything used is CC0 (public domain). Downloaded 2026-11-06 without any account, login or captcha; licence text kept next to each
file in `source/`. Nothing else third-party is included; everything not listed here is generated by the scripts in this folder.

## Quaternius, Ultimate Animated Animals pack (CC0)
- Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com). Pack page: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html
  (files from the pack's public Google Drive folder, FBX variants).
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, see `source/quaternius/ultimate_animated_animals/License.txt`.
- Used files (about 3.0-3.8 MB each, 17 MB total): `Wolf.fbx` (-> wolf, and base for bear, boar, dragon), `ShibaInu.fbx` (-> dog),
  `Fox.fbx` (-> fox, and base for hare), `Horse.fbx` (-> horse, and the horse half of the centaur), `Deer.fbx` (-> deer, and base for goat).
- Modified by us: re-coloured to the family palette, re-proportioned (rest-pose bone scaling + mesh inflation) for bear/boar/goat/hare/dragon,
  extra parts, smooth normals, real-world scale, clips renamed and trimmed.

## Garden of Eden base body (humanoid monsters, harpy, centaur torso)
- `source/human_base/` is a snapshot (2026-11-06) of `art3d/characters/export/` (`base_body.fbx`, `base_body_meta.json`,
  `joint_offsets.json`, `species_presets.json`), which was built from the Blender Studio Human Base Meshes bundle v1.4.1
  (CC0, see `art3d/characters/CREDITS.md`). Copied, not edited. Size about 4.2 MB.

## Considered and not used
- Quaternius Ultimate Monsters (Orc, Yeti, Bunny, Birb, Pigeon, Dragon_Fly), Animated Monster Pack Dragon, Farm Animals (Pig, Sheep, Llama):
  CC0 and downloadable, but chibi / atlas-textured or very low poly (about 500 tris) and clash with the art direction, so not shipped.
- Poly Pizza bears/goats (mostly CC-BY, "Poly by Google"): static meshes without rigs, not used.
Total added under `art3d/creatures/` (sources + exports + previews): about 130 MB.
