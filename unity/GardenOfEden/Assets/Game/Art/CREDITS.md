## World: ground, trees, bushes, rocks (2026-11-22 world pass)

No downloaded assets. The CC0 nature packs that fit the budget (Quaternius, Kenney, KayKit) are faceted low-poly, which the
G8 art direction rules out (smooth matte, soft gradients), so the world is code-built: soft-shaded trees, bushes and rocks in
`World/PropLibrary.cs` (`MeshBuilder.SoftBlob` / `SoftTube` in `World/MeshKit.cs`), painterly ground breakup, snow cover,
foliage wind and translucency in `Look/Shaders/ToonLit.shader`, rain and snow in `Look/WeatherParticles.cs`. All project's own.
